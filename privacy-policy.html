Privacy Policy

StackNova · Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StackNova ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information when you play our browser game. StackNova is developed and operated as an independent project. By playing the Game, you agree to the practices described below.

1. The short version

StackNova runs entirely in your browser. We do not require an account, we do not operate a backend server that stores your gameplay, and we do not collect your name, email, or contact details. Your best score is saved locally on your own device. Third-party advertising partners (such as Google) may collect technical data as described in Section 3.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Best score — stored in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.
  • Cookie consent choice — stored in local storage so we remember whether you accepted or declined non-essential cookies.

3. Third-party advertising and cookies

StackNova is free to play and supported by advertising. Advertising may be served by Google (AdSense / H5 Games Ads) and by the platform hosting the Game (for example, CrazyGames). These partners are independent controllers of the data they collect and may use cookies and similar technologies.

WhoWhat they may collectPurpose
Google (AdSense / H5 Games Ads)IP address, device and browser identifiers, ad interaction data, cookiesServing and measuring ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention
Hosting platform (e.g. CrazyGames)IP address, device and browser identifiers, gameplay analyticsPlatform operation, ad delivery, aggregate analytics

Where required by law (including the EU/EEA and UK), non-essential cookies and ad personalization are only activated after you give consent through the cookie banner shown in the Game.

You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google by visiting Google Ads Settings, or from multiple providers at aboutads.info/choices and networkadvertising.org.

4. Analytics

The Game itself does not include any first-party analytics SDK. Aggregate, non-identifying gameplay statistics (such as number of levels completed) may be provided to us by the hosting platform for the purpose of improving the Game.

5. Your rights under GDPR (EU/EEA/UK)

If you are located in the EU/EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port personal data concerning you, and to object to or withdraw consent for its processing. Because we do not operate user accounts, most requests concern data held by our advertising partners; contact them directly for data about you held in their systems. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Legal bases: consent (cookie banner) for non-essential cookies and personalized advertising; legitimate interests for basic operation, security, and fraud prevention.

6. Your rights under CCPA (California)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, to correct inaccurate data, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Advertising partners may use cookies for cross-context behavioral advertising; you can opt out via the links in Section 3. We do not discriminate against users who exercise their privacy rights.

7. Children's privacy

StackNova is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Data retention and security

Local data (best score, consent choice) remains on your device until you clear it. Data processed by advertising partners is retained under their own policies. We implement reasonable technical measures appropriate to a browser-based game, but no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

9. International transfers

Advertising partners may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Such transfers are governed by the partners' own privacy policies and applicable transfer mechanisms.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: hello@blackgames.cn

隐私政策

StackNova · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 24 日

本隐私政策说明 StackNova（"本游戏"、"我们"）在您游玩这款浏览器游戏时如何处理信息。StackNova 是一个独立开发、独立运营的项目。您游玩本游戏，即表示同意本政策所述的做法。

一、简版说明

StackNova 完全在您的浏览器中运行。我们不要求注册账户，不运营存储您游戏数据的后端服务器，也不收集您的姓名、邮箱或联系方式。您的最高分仅保存在您自己的设备上。第三方广告合作伙伴（如 Google）可能收集技术数据，详见第三节。

二、存储在您设备上的信息

  • 最高分——存储于浏览器的本地存储（localStorage）。该数据不会离开您的设备，您可随时通过浏览器设置清除。
  • Cookie 同意选择——存储于本地存储，用于记住您接受还是拒绝了非必要 Cookie。

三、第三方广告与 Cookie

StackNova 免费畅玩，由广告收入支持。广告可能由 Google（AdSense / H5 Games Ads）以及托管本游戏的平台（如 CrazyGames）提供。这些合作伙伴是其收集数据的独立控制者，可能使用 Cookie 及类似技术。

主体可能收集的数据用途
Google（AdSense / H5 Games Ads）IP 地址、设备与浏览器标识符、广告交互数据、Cookie广告投放与效果衡量、频次控制、反欺诈
托管平台（如 CrazyGames）IP 地址、设备与浏览器标识符、游戏行为分析平台运营、广告投放、聚合分析

在法律要求的情况下（包括欧盟/欧洲经济区和英国），非必要 Cookie 与广告个性化仅在您通过游戏内 Cookie 横幅给予同意后才会启用。

您可以通过 Google 广告设置 退出 Google 的个性化广告，或访问 aboutads.info/choices 与 networkadvertising.org 退出多家提供商的个性化广告。

四、分析工具

本游戏自身不包含任何第一方分析 SDK。托管平台可能向我们提供聚合的、不识别个人身份的游戏统计数据（如关卡完成次数），用于改进游戏。

五、GDPR（欧盟/欧洲经济区/英国）下的权利

如果您位于欧盟/欧洲经济区或英国，您有权访问、更正、删除、限制或移植与您相关的个人数据，并有权反对处理或撤回同意。由于我们不运营用户账户，多数请求涉及广告合作伙伴持有的数据，请直接联系他们查询其系统中的数据。您亦有权向当地监管机构投诉。

法律基础：非必要 Cookie 与个性化广告基于您的同意（Cookie 横幅）；基础运营、安全与反欺诈基于正当利益。

六、CCPA（加利福尼亚州）下的权利

加州居民有权知悉收集了哪些个人信息、请求删除、更正不准确数据，以及选择退出个人信息的"出售"或"共享"。我们不出售个人信息。广告合作伙伴可能将 Cookie 用于跨场景行为广告，您可通过第三节的链接退出。我们不会因您行使隐私权而歧视您。

七、儿童隐私

StackNova 不面向 13 岁以下儿童（或您所在国家/地区规定的最低年龄）。我们不会在知情的情况下收集儿童的个人信息。如您认为有儿童通过本游戏提供了个人信息，请与我们联系，以便采取适当措施。

八、数据保留与安全

本地数据（最高分、同意选择）保留在您的设备上，直至您主动清除。广告合作伙伴处理的数据按其自身政策保留。我们针对浏览器游戏实施了合理的技术措施，但互联网传输没有绝对安全的方式。

九、国际传输

广告合作伙伴可能在您所在国家/地区以外的国家（包括美国）处理数据。此类传输受合作伙伴自身隐私政策及适用的传输机制约束。
